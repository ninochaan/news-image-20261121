A newspaper front page that sums up Saturday 21 November 2026 in a single
image — 44 stories: two tankers struck in the Strait of Hormuz, Verstappen
wins a wild Las Vegas Grand Prix, BepiColombo reaches Mercury after eight
years, a food-truck strike kills three aid workers in Gaza, France marches
for a sixth Saturday, Bitcoin dives to $55,130 then retakes $60,000, the EU
and Indonesia sign a landmark trade deal, COP31 drags into a second day of
overtime, the St. Johns River breaks its 1953 flood record, a candy factory
fire kills 3 and leaves 36 missing in Banten, drones set a Russian cargo
ship ablaze in the Gulf of Finland, a magnitude 6.8 quake shakes Maluku,
Bolsonaro lands in intensive care, UN killer-robot talks end with no binding
rules, the Princess of Wales reveals her cancer film "Heal", and Malen hits
four in Roma's 5-1 rout — plus 28 numbered one-line briefs, a "WHERE IT
HAPPENED" world map, a big-numbers stat strip, and a DATE CHECK box naming
the big stories left out because they happened on another day.

Every headline is a verified 21 November 2026 event (sources in
`../dev/STORY-POOL.md` of the build workspace); one-party claims stay
attributed. The image is drawn deterministically by `gen.py` (Pillow, no
network, no timestamps, no randomness) and written to `entry.png`
(1024×1024). `main.ts` copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
